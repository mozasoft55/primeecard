Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We are here to make your wedding invitation seamless.

How do I send this invitation to our guests on WhatsApp?

You receive your unique invitation link along with a pre-written WhatsApp message. You can send individual personalized guest links or share to your family WhatsApp groups. When guests tap the link, it opens instantly in full royal beauty!

Can I personalize the invitation card with each guest's name?

Yes! With our Premium Royale and Imperial packages, each guest or family receives a personalized card featuring their own name (e.g. "Dear Sharma Family" or "Dear Amit & Neha").

Can I edit dates, venues, or timings after ordering?

Yes, absolutely! Unlike printed cards where mistakes cost thousands of rupees, our digital invitations can be updated anytime in seconds. Your guests will always see the latest updated details.

Do our guests need to download any mobile application?

No! Zero apps required. The invitation opens immediately inside WhatsApp, Safari, Chrome, or any smartphone browser with 1-second loading speed.

How does the live RSVP and guest tracking work?

Guests tap "RSVP Now", specify how many family members are attending, and leave dietary notes. You receive these responses instantly in your live dashboard and via WhatsApp.

What is your turnaround time?

Your draft is ready in 30 minutes, and your final live link is delivered in under 1 hour!

How long does my wedding invitation link remain active?

Your wedding link stays active for 1 full year after your wedding date, allowing you and your guests to revisit memories and view wedding photos.
